Montgomery

State Capital

General Information

  • Population: 192,112
  • Incorporation Year: 1819
  • Region: Central
  • City classification: Urban
  • Income vs. State Average:$41,474 / 60,786

Montgomery has an estimated population of 192,117 residents, as the state capital, following the 2020 state census, with an annual decline of 0.82%. The city has an average income per person of $41,474 per year compared to the state average of $60,786 per person.

Since its incorporation in 1819, Montgomery has had a significant involvement in our nation's history, being both the first capital of the Confederate army and a central hub of the civil rights movement. Today, it is an urban city in the central Alabama region, serving as both a cultural and historical landmark. Montgomery provides many entertainment options, such as the Alabama Shakespeare Festival, the Civil Rights Memorial Center, and Fort Toulouse- Jackson Park.
