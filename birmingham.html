Birmingham

General Information

  • Population: 194,156
  • Incorporation Year: 1871
  • Region: North Central
  • City classification: Urban
  • Income vs. State Average: $38,326 / $43,984

Birmingham has an estimated population of 194,156 residents following the 2020 state census, with an annual decrease of 0.64%. The city has an average income per person of $38,326 per year compared to the state average of $43,984 per year.(World Population Review)

Founded in 1871 by merging three communities (Wikipedia), Birmingham has been a central industrial hub in Alabama. Today, Birmingham has incorporated an array of cultural attractions and Entertainment such as the Alabama Ballet, the Birmingham Museum of Art, and the BJCC Arena.
